Privacy in the Android app

SunCalc is published by Matija Radeljak. No SunCalc account, advertising, advertising identifier, or behavioral analytics is required. The hosted web planner has separate terms.

Location and the compass

Only the location and Live Shadow buttons request foreground location. Approximate location is sufficient; precise location can improve the pin. The free dial calculates on the device and does not send its location or heading to SunCalc. Sensors stop when you leave the dial or the app. You can turn permission off in Android settings.

Planning

Saved places and your last pin stay in app storage. To draw online maps, the app requests numbered map tiles from suncalc.app; these requests indicate the map area being viewed. The app caches recently viewed tiles. Town search is built in. If you submit a global address lookup, SunCalc sends the submitted text to Geoapify. A shared link includes the coordinates, place name and time; only share it with people or apps you choose.

Trial and Google Play

The app records its first open. When connected, it sends the start time and an app-scoped pseudonymous device value to SunCalc over HTTPS. The server stores a keyed digest and the earliest start so reinstalling does not renew the seven-day trial. No serial number, advertising ID or GPS history is used for this purpose.

Google Play handles payments. To validate and restore purchases, the app sends a Play purchase token and product ID to SunCalc. SunCalc verifies the token with Google, and retains a token digest, product, entitlement, expiry and a link to the app installation. SunCalc does not receive card details. Google Play processes information under Google’s privacy policy. Cloudflare processes the network requests and hosts SunCalc’s entitlement and map services.

Retention and contact

Device settings and pins can be removed by clearing app data. Trial records are retained to remember the trial; purchase records are retained to provide restoration and process cancellations and refunds. For privacy questions or requests for deletion, contact Matija Radeljak. Do not post device values or purchase receipts in a public issue. Manage or cancel your subscription in Google Play; clearing data does not cancel it.
