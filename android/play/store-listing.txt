Title: SunCalc: Sun & Shadow Planner
Short description: Plan sunlight anywhere and follow a live shadow compass wherever you stand.

Full description:
Where will the sun set behind that building? Drop a pin, choose a day, and scrub the local clock to see the sun’s direction and the shadow an upright object would cast on level ground.

Compare solstices, check twilight and golden light, save places on your device, and share a moment with a precise link. Solar calculations, town search, an overview map of the world and detailed streets around Split are included for offline use. New detailed maps elsewhere need an internet connection.

For a live reading, hold your phone flat. Live Shadow uses the current sun and your phone’s compass to show the direction and length of a one-metre post’s shadow. No camera is needed. It stays free after the trial.

The Android planner includes seven full days from first open. No card and no automatic charge. After the trial, choose a monthly subscription or one lifetime purchase at the price shown by Google Play. Monthly subscriptions renew until canceled; manage them or restore purchases in Google Play. Saved places stay on the device when the trial ends. The app models a clear, level horizon; it does not simulate shade from buildings, hills or trees.
