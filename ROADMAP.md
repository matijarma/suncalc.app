# Roadmap

SunCalc.app focuses on simple sun direction, timing and level-ground shadows.
The hosted planner is free during preview. The planned paid release is 30 full
days, then €1 once per browser installation; today's sunrise and sunset stay free.

## Current

- A map-first planner, saved places, precise sharing and an offline app shell.
- Sun position, sunrise/sunset, golden-light events and seasonal comparisons.
- A height-based shadow ruler.
- Public MIT-licensed source and practical guides.

## Next

- Learn from real-world feedback, accessibility checks and search queries.
- Publish a December-solstice demonstration.
- Explore locally generated, downloadable plan images with map attribution.
- Complete the documented payment validation before enabling paid access.

## After evidence of demand

- A small collection of useful city-specific pages.
- Translated guides and interface copy.
- Educational examples for teachers and outdoor planning.

Terrain, buildings and tree-shade simulation are not part of the current model.
Suggestions are welcome in [Issues](https://github.com/matijarma/suncalc.app/issues).
